---
name: remember-brand
description: Save a durable brand preference for future One Take video generation.
argument-hint: <brand preference>
---

Store the user's brand preference in Workspace Memory using Miniclaw's memory capability.

Prefer structured facts for:

- brand tone
- banned phrases
- preferred CTA
- typography or pacing constraints
- audience and channel constraints

Do not store One Take project status, task IDs, or generated media in Memory. One Take remains the source of truth for project and media state.

Confirm the exact remembered preference back to the user.
